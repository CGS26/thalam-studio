# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Tāla Lab contributors are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Respect privacy and cultural context.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and correct mistakes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruptive conduct
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the process in `SECURITY.md`, marking
the report as a community conduct concern. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project
publicly.
